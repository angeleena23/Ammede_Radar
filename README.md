### Project Description
Amma Radar is a humorous, culturally accurate household comedy web application that calculates an Indian mother's ("Amma's") anger level based on a curfew questionnaire and subjects late-arriving children to an escalating series of comedic punishments.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Teens and young adults returning home past their curfew have absolutely no way of quantitatively measuring the exact velocity, tactical readiness, or weapon selection of their mother's impending wrath.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
A panic-inducing, interactive web app featuring dynamic weapon-floating animations, a 10-question anxiety-driven interrogation engine, live background color grading, and video-based cutscene punishments ranging from a gentle stare to high-velocity kitchen utensils!

### Project Demo
# Video
https://drive.google.com/file/d/1xji4uB8y9KZu4FwBteb9GwJrneBiduIl/view?usp=sharing
The video demonstrates the complete user journey through the Ammede Radar web application:

1. Curfew and Demographic Input: The user enters their profile details, selection parameters, entry time, and late duration on the configuration screen.
2. Dynamic 10-Question Interrogation: The user navigates through a series of anxiety-driven situational questions with "Yes/No" options regarding curfew violations, phone ignoring habits, and alibis.
3. Anger Level Grading: As answers accumulate, the interface background shifts dynamically from blue to intense red, signaling an escalating danger level.
4. Final Verdict and Punishment Cutscene: The app calculates a high anger score (Level 9), displaying a video cutscene of a high-velocity chappal throw accompanied by the iconic warning punchline (*"Nee theernada.. nee theernu"*), before offering an option to return to the home screen.
